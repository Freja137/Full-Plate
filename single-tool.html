A GOOD SHARP KNIFE

By Mads, Professional Chef

Discover practical cooking tips, helpful ingredient advice and trusted kitchen tools recommended by real cooks.

16. September 2026

Some ingredients arrive in the kitchen with a story already attached to them. Fish is one of them. Long before it reaches the plate, it has travelled from open water to fishing boat, from harbour to market, and finally into the hands of the person preparing it. For me, cooking with fish has always been about respecting that journey. The best dishes often begin with very little: something fresh from the sea, a hot pan, a little acidity and ingredients that support rather than hide the flavour.
